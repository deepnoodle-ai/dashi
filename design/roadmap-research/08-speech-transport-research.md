# Speech transport — research

Status: source survey, 2026-09-28. Roadmap [item 8](../remaining-work.md). This distinguishes **audio content** in an LLM message, a **batch speech endpoint** for recognition or synthesis, a **speech stream** with incremental audio/text, and a **live voice session** with two-way turn-taking. The projects do not offer the same capability just because each represents audio. `tmp/research/comparison/` provided orientation; the locations below were checked in source. No live speech calls were made.

## Dashi baseline

`speech/speech.go:1-16,27-83,102-174` defines validated `TranscribeInput`, `SynthesizeInput`, `Transcription`, and `Synthesis`, but no client or transport. Recognition can legitimately return empty text for silence; synthesis yields a `content.Block`, not a playback or durable-storage claim. `speech/capabilities.go:10-55` keeps batch, audio streaming, and incremental text support separate and scopes claims through `ModelInfo`. `content/content.go:26-28,36-73,218-240` carries audio sources and format without fetching them. The [media and speech proposal](../media-and-speech-proposal.md) proposes Prepare → Send batch calls, distinct speech streams, and a separate realtime package; it remains a proposal. Existing `media/request.go:223-245` is an example of explicit prepared calls, though speech multipart uploads need their own bounded plan.

## Lessons and tradeoffs

1. **Make the operation explicit.** Dive's dedicated endpoint calls give callers a direct file → transcript or text → audio flow. Eino/MAF audio blocks support a different job: letting an LLM reason over media. A Dashi `Recognizer` and `Synthesizer` should return typed speech results rather than repurposing an `llm.Message` response.
2. **Prepare exact behavior before dispatch.** OpenAI multipart ASR and Gemini prompted ASR differ materially. Model/API routing, accepted source type, audio MIME, language, timing, voice, format, and any fallback must be visible or rejected before remote work. Unsupported options cannot quietly disappear. The requested and served model should survive in the result.
3. **Treat silence and transport failures correctly.** An empty provider transcript can mean no detected speech; it should not automatically be `ErrNoResult`. Uploaded bytes, response size, cancellation, and uncertain dispatch need bounded/observable handling. Generated audio is data; the application owns storage and playback.
4. **Keep streams distinct from live voice.** A one-way stream can yield partial/final transcripts or audio chunks without a conversation runtime. ADK's Gemini Live path includes bidirectional turn-taking and reconnection concerns; folding it into simple speech calls would burden the common batch UX. Specify ordering, finalization, and cancellation against at least two provider shapes before committing public stream interfaces.
