# 19. Durable retries after proven rejection — source research

**Status:** Draft research, 2026-09-28. **Roadmap:** [item 19](../remaining-work.md), M / high. **Question:** When may Dashi retry a model request after a failure without guessing whether the first request ran?

## Dashi baseline

`llm.Client.Send` makes at most one dispatch; its caller owns attempt identity, recording and retry (`llm/llm.go:584-610`). The Runner records a prepared request and admission before dispatch, using a distinct `<turn>:model:<n>` attempt ID (`runner.go:1143-1236`). Settlement counts a rejected attempt, records failure evidence and charge, and currently stops failed on known nonexecution or uncertain on unknown effect (`runner.go:1268-1320`; `state.go:505-560`). `Settle.ModelFailure` can later reconcile an uncertain attempt with host-verified evidence (`runner.go:479-507`). `BeforeModel`, `BeforeSend`, budget and deadline checks are part of each new call; retry must pass them again.

`llm.Error` separates `Disposition` from advisory `Retryable` and `RetryAfter` (`llm/llm.go:665-713`). This matters: the shared HTTP adapter marks only selected 4xx responses as `FailureRejected`; 408, 409, 425 **and all 5xx** remain `FailureUnknown` (`internal/providerhttp/http.go:286-305,330-336`). Its `shouldRetry` marks 429 and several 5xx as *possibly transient* (`internal/providerhttp/errors.go:113-126`). Thus 429 can be a proven rejection with a retry hint; a generic 503 has a retry hint but **does not authorize automatic replay**. The roadmap's “rejected 429 or 503” wording is broader than the code. Partial result, output or usage also forces unknown disposition (`runner.go:1324-1334`). No wait/due-time fact exists; rejected 429 ends `StopFailed`. An ordinary `Continue` after failure is possible, but requires the host to invent scheduling and retry eligibility.

## Lessons and decision pressure

1. **Proof and transient advice are separate.** Dashi should require both `FailureRejected` and `Retryable`, plus no partial response. A 503, timeout, dropped socket, or stream break stays uncertain until provider-specific or host evidence says otherwise. Provider-hosted effects need extra caution even for an HTTP error.
2. **One layer owns the retry.** Nvoken explicitly disabled Dive retries; pi disables SDK auto-retries before its wrapper. A Dashi host must avoid hidden transport/SDK retries or its attempt IDs, budgets and evidence cease to describe actual calls.
3. **Persist the wait, not a sleeping worker.** pi demonstrates a recorded `notBefore` and host-visible waiting outcome. Dashi can expose this through its existing Turn/`Continue` vocabulary; it need not become a job scheduler.
4. **Every attempt re-enters admission.** A later worker must recheck cancellation, deadline, attempt and spend limits, `BeforeModel`, request preparation and `BeforeSend`. The next attempt gets a new ID and digest. `Retry-After` is a lower bound, not a reason to exceed a configured wait/deadline silently.
