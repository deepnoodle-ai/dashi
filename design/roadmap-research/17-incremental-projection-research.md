# 17. Incremental projection — source research

**Status:** Draft research, 2026-09-28. **Roadmap:** [item 17](../remaining-work.md), M / medium. **Question:** Is reconstructing Dashi's Turn view and model messages becoming expensive enough to justify a derived cache?

## Dashi baseline

`Replay(Turn)` calls `replay`, which validates and JSON-serializes every step, applies transitions, then derives pending work, answer and spend (`state.go:123-205`). `Project(Turn)` calls `replay` again and walks the steps to choose current context snapshots, the last model intent, settled assistant/tool pairs and hosted approvals (`project.go:10-185`). `Runner` invokes replay at entry, for current outcomes and at other transitions, and calls `projectWithHistory` before model requests (`runner.go:225,772,794,962,1039,1313`); `MemoryRecorder.Commit` also replays the entire updated Turn (`recorder.go:239`). Work across a long Turn can therefore grow faster than its step count. The exact wall time and share of local CPU are **unmeasured** in this research; provider time, tool time, full request serialization and cloning may dominate.

The step record is authoritative. `Start.History` / `Continue.History` are caller-owned and not in the Turn ([decision 19](../decisions.md)); a cache must not become a second copy of that conversation. A new settled tool call can make an earlier assistant tool call projectable, while a later named `ContextItem.Snapshot` supersedes earlier context and `NextCallOnly` expires after an intent (`project.go:51-101,108-164`). A cache cannot safely assume each new step only appends one message.

## Lessons and decision pressure

1. **Measure first.** Dashi's repeated replay is evident from call sites, but no profile says it is a meaningful bottleneck. Full prompt messages must still be prepared and sent, so eliminating interpretation may not change end-to-end latency.
2. **Cache by acknowledged revision.** An in-process view is useful only when tied to an exact Turn ID and step sequence, updated after a successful recorder acknowledgement. Failed/uncertain commits, a new `Run`, cold load or foreign owner invalidate it. Replay from facts must remain the fallback.
3. **Projection changes retroactively.** Context snapshots, tool-call/result pairing, interrupted results, hosted approvals and compaction rules make naive tail append wrong. OpenHands explicitly falls back to full enforcement after divergence; Dashi should retain a cold `Replay`/`Project` oracle for equivalence.
4. **Do not persist a second truth.** Nvoken and Mobius checkpoints serve recovery/coverage, while pi and ADK compaction records alter *which facts* the model sees. A performance cache should neither contain business decisions nor repair records, and should not appear in the public API.
